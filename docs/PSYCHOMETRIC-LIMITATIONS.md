# 心理計量邊界與局限性宣告
# Psychometric Boundaries & Limitations Charter

---

## 繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese)

### 1. 測量模型性質與非常模限制 (Ipsative Nature & Absence of Norms)
* **自比型迫選架構（Ipsative Model）**：本工具採用強迫選擇（Forced-Choice）設計，測量受測者在四個維度（D/I/S/C）之間的**相對偏好與資源取捨**，並非測量特質的絕對強度或能力水平。
* **無跨人橫向可比性（No Cross-Individual Comparability）**：本系統不包含常模群體（Normative Benchmark），各維度得分僅反映個人內部的優先級排列。將兩人得分進行數值橫向對比在心理計量學上是不成立且無效的。
* **非高利害決策用途（Non-High-Stakes Use）**：嚴禁直接用於招聘甄選、晉升裁定、績效考核等高利害（High-Stakes）人事決策。本工具僅供自我覺察、領導力教練對話及團隊協作反思使用。

### 2. 跨語言測量等價性限制 (Cross-Lingual Measurement Inequivalence)
* **未經雙向回譯程序（No Formal Back-Translation）**：繁體中文與英文題本係由作者依構念對等原則直接撰寫，尚未經過嚴格之正向／反向回譯（Forward-Backward Back-Translation）及專家審查委員會流程。
* **語義漂移（Semantic Shift）**：部分詞彙在不同文化與語境下存在細微語義偏差。中英兩套題本所得分數不可直接視為計量等價（Metric Equivalence 未經檢驗），跨語言比較需保持審慎。

### 3. 提問框架與方法變異 (Framing & Common Method Variance)
* **混合提問框架（Mixed Item Stems）**：題本中混合了「行為傾向（Behavioral Tendency）」、「價值優先級（Value Priority）」及「自我概念（Self-Concept）」三類題幹框架。
* **框架效應（Framing Effects）**：尚未透過多構念-多方法矩陣（MTMM）或結構方程模型（SEM）檢驗以排除方法變異（Method Variance）。在 Phase 2 實證校準完成前，本工具之產出僅作反思提示，不作構念診斷。

### 4. 注意力檢驗設計 (Attention Check Design)
* **單向注意力檢查（Single-Barreled Check）**：第 25 題為定向指令題，僅要求受測者將指定選項標記為「最符合（Most）」，未對「最不符（Least）」進行要求與計分。
* **探索性容錯設計（Non-Blocking Design）**：未通過注意力檢查者，系統將在報告頂部顯示有效性提示橫幅，但不會中斷報告產出，以維持非正式探索場景下之回饋體驗。

## English

### 1. Measurement Model & Absence of Normative Standards
* **Ipsative Forced-Choice Architecture**: This instrument employs a forced-choice format to measure an individual's **relative behavioral preferences and internal trade-offs** across four dimensions (D/I/S/C), rather than absolute trait intensity or competency levels.
* **Lack of Cross-Individual Comparability**: No normative sample or standardized distribution is utilized. Dimension scores reflect purely intra-individual preference hierarchies; comparing raw or shifted numerical scores between different individuals is psychometrically invalid.
* **Non-High-Stakes Application**: This instrument is strictly restricted from being used in high-stakes personnel decisions (e.g., pre-employment screening, performance appraisals, promotion gates). It is intended exclusively for personal reflection, 1-on-1 coaching dialogue, and team collaboration workshops.

### 2. Cross-Lingual Measurement Equivalence
* **Absence of Formal Back-Translation**: The Traditional Chinese and English item banks were developed directly based on construct-alignment principles by the author, without undergoing a formal multi-stage forward-backward back-translation protocol by an independent review committee.
* **Semantic Drift**: Subtle connotative shifts may exist across cultural and linguistic contexts. Metric and scalar equivalence between language versions have not been empirically verified. Cross-linguistic score comparisons should be interpreted with caution.

### 3. Item Framing & Method Variance
* **Mixed Item Stems**: The item bank intentionally combines three distinct cognitive frames: "Behavioral Tendency", "Value Priority", and "Self-Concept".
* **Method Variance**: Potential framing effects have not yet been evaluated via Multitrait-Multimethod (MTMM) matrices or confirmatory factor analysis (CFA). Prior to Phase 2 empirical validation, results serve solely as reflective heuristics rather than diagnostic profiling.

### 4. Attention Check Architecture
* **Single-Barreled Verification**: Item 25 serves as a directed-instruction check, requiring the respondent to mark a specific option as "Most". No "Least" selection is required or scored.
* **Non-Blocking Observability**: Failure to follow the attention check triggers a visible validity notice banner atop the report, but does not abort report generation, thereby preserving exploratory feedback continuity.
